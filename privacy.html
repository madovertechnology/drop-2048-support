Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

2048 Merge: Drop Number Balls is provided by Mad Over Technology. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@madovertech.io.

Information kept on your device

The game stores your age category (under 18 or adult), game progress, best score, sound settings, and Remove Ads status on your device. The birth date entered at the start is used to determine the age category and is not saved. The game has no player account or separate game-data server. Removing the app removes local data, subject to your device backup settings.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob for ads. Google may process device and app information, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to provide, measure, and protect ads. The game requests non-personalized ads. It applies child-directed treatment and a G-rated content limit for younger players, and does not show them full-screen ads. Live ad requests use Google's consent flow where required. Adult players can open ad privacy choices in Settings when available. See Google's privacy policy.

Optional gameplay analytics

For adult players only, Settings offers an optional “Help improve the game” switch, off by default. When enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives events such as a game start, highest number reached, round score and drop count, and a board share. Google may also receive an app instance identifier, device and app information, and diagnostics to provide analytics. The game does not send your name or entered birth date to analytics. Turn the switch off at any time to stop future collection. Analytics stays off for younger players. See Google’s privacy policy.

Optional purchase

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase handled by Apple or Google. The store processes payment details and provides purchase status so the game can remove ads and restore ownership. We do not receive payment card details.

Other device features

The game can play music and sound. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise location. Sharing a score uses your device's share sheet only when you choose to share.

Your choices

You can turn music, sound, and optional adult gameplay analytics on or off in Settings. You can use ad privacy choices when available, restore an owned Remove Ads purchase, and contact us at hello@madovertech.io about privacy or data requests.
